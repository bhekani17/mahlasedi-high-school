Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Mahlasedi High School

Admissions Academics Fees Uniform

Admissions

1

How do I apply to Mahlasedi High School?

You can apply online through our website by filling out the application form or visit our admissions office. The application process includes submitting required documents, attending an interview if necessary, and paying the registration fee.

2

What are the admission requirements?

Requirements include: completed application form, birth certificate, previous school reports, parent/guardian ID, proof of residence, and payment of the R1,500 registration fee.

3

When is the application deadline?

Applications for the next academic year close on November 30th. Late applications may be considered based on availability.

4

Is there an entrance exam?

Yes, applicants may be required to take an entrance assessment depending on the grade level and previous academic performance.

Academics

1

What curriculum does the school follow?

We follow the South African National Curriculum Statement (CAPS) for Grades R-12, ensuring alignment with national educational standards.

2

What subjects are offered?

We offer core subjects including Mathematics, English, Science, Social Sciences, and additional subjects like Computer Studies, Business Studies, and Arts.

3

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?

We maintain a student-to-teacher ratio of approximately 35:1, ensuring personalized attention for each learner.

4

Does the school offer extra lessons?

Yes, we offer after-school extra lessons for subjects like Mathematics, Science, and English at no additional cost.

School Fees

1

What are the school fees?

Primary School (Grades 0-7): R700/month. Secondary School (Grades 8-12): R1,100-R1,500/month depending on grade level.

2

Is there a registration fee?

Yes, there is a one-time registration fee of R1,500 for all new students.

3

Are there any hidden costs?

No, our fees are all-inclusive. There are no hidden charges for textbooks or basic stationery.

4

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer monthly payment plans and can discuss flexible payment options with parents who need assistance.

Uniform

1

Where can I buy school uniforms?

Uniforms can be purchased from our on-campus uniform store during school hours or online through our website.

2

What does the uniform consist of?

The uniform includes school shirts, trousers/skirts, school badge, and appropriate footwear. Specific items vary by season and grade level.

3

Is the uniform mandatory?

Yes, wearing the complete school uniform is mandatory for all students during school hours and school events.

4

How much does the uniform cost?

Complete uniform sets range from R450-R580 depending on grade level and season. Individual items are also available.

General

1

What are the school hours?

School runs from 07:30 to 14:30, Monday to Friday. After-school programs and extra lessons may extend until 16:00.

2

Does the school have transport?

We don't provide school transport, but we can assist parents in organizing carpool groups in certain areas.

3

What sports and activities are offered?

We offer soccer, netball, athletics, cricket, and various cultural activities. Students participate in inter-school competitions.

4

How can I contact the school?

You can call us at +27 11 402 0040, email info@mahlasedihighschool.co.za, or visit us at 181 Moritz Ave, Halfway House, Midrand.

5

Does the school have a parent portal?

Yes, our parent portal allows parents to track student progress, view reports, and communicate with teachers.
