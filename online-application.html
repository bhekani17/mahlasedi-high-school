Application Requirements

Primary School (Grades 0-7)

Age Requirements: Grade 0 - 5 years turning 6 by 30 June
Documents Needed: Birth certificate, immunization card, last school report (if applicable)
Assessment: Placement assessment may be required
Fees: R700/month + R1,500 registration fee

Secondary School (Grades 8-12)

Academic Requirements: Previous year report card with minimum 50% average
Documents Needed: Birth certificate, ID, last 2 years school reports, transfer card
Assessment: Entrance examination and interview required
Fees: R1,100-R1,500/month + R1,500 registration fee
