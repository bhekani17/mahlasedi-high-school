Uniform Guidelines

General Requirements

  • Complete uniform must be worn during school hours
  • Uniforms must be clean, neat, and in good condition
  • No alterations to uniform design or style
  • Proper black school shoes required
  • School badges must be visible at all times

Appearance Standards

  • Natural hair color only
  • Simple, neat hairstyles
  • No jewelry except watches
  • No makeup or nail polish
  • No visible tattoos or piercings
