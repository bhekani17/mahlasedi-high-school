Admissions

Join Mahlasedi High School and embark on a journey of academic excellence.

Application Process

1

Inquiry

Contact us or visit our campus to learn more about our school and programs.

2

Application

Complete the application form and submit required documents.

3

Assessment

Learner assessment and parent interview to ensure good fit.

4

Enrollment

Receive acceptance letter and complete enrollment process.

Admission Requirements

Required Documents

  • Certified copy of learner's birth certificate
  • Certified copy of parent/guardian's ID
  • Latest school report (if applicable)
  • Transfer card (if transferring)
  • 2 passport-size photos
  • Proof of residence
  • Registration fee payment

Admission Criteria

  • Age-appropriate for grade level
  • Satisfactory academic performance
  • Good conduct record
  • Commitment to school values and policies
  • Space availability in desired grade

Important Information

Application Period

Applications for the upcoming academic year typically open in Term 3 (July-September). Early application is encouraged as spaces are limited.

Grade Availability

We accept applications for Grades 0 through 12. Each grade has limited capacity to ensure quality education.

  • Grade 0-7: Primary education
  • Grade 8: Entry level for high school
  • Grades 9-11: Mid-phase transfers
  • Grade 12: Limited availability

Application Fee

A non-refundable application fee is required with submission. Contact our office for current fee structure and payment methods.

School Tours

We encourage prospective families to schedule a campus tour. Contact us to arrange a visit during school hours.
