About Us

Learn more about Mahlasedi High School's mission, vision, and values.

Our Mission

To provide quality secondary education that empowers learners in Grades 8–12 to achieve academic excellence, develop critical thinking skills, and become responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To be a leading secondary school in Gauteng, recognized for academic excellence, holistic development, and preparing learners for success in higher education and beyond.

Our Core Values

Excellence

We strive for academic excellence and continuous improvement in all aspects of education.

Integrity

We uphold honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct in all our interactions.

Respect

We value diversity and treat everyone with dignity, kindness, and understanding.

Innovation

We embrace modern teaching methods and technology to enhance learning experiences.

Care

We provide a nurturing environment where every learner feels valued and supported.

Discipline

We maintain high standards of behavior and accountability to foster a productive learning environment.

Our Story

Mahlasedi High School is an integral pillar of the educational landscape in Johannesburg, serving as a bustling hub of intellectual growth and development. Located at 181 Moritz Ave, Halfway House, Midrand, our school serves 990 learners from Grade 8 through Grade 12.

Operating under the Johannesburg East Education District, we are committed to providing a structured learning environment that aligns with the Gauteng Department of Education curriculum while fostering individual growth and development.

Our dedicated team of 28 qualified educators works tirelessly to ensure that every learner receives the support and guidance needed to excel academically and prepare for their future endeavors.
